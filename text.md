The goal of this project is to highlight how chatbots can co-participate in a user's improper or vulnerable usage, validating and reinforcing harmful behaviors that ultimately endanger the user or others. The gravity and weight of these real-world consequences constitute where the materiality of this technology lies, moving beyond the abstract and the virtuality of it to tangible impact. "AI Induced Psychosis" is a single-page glyph-based matrix that brings together our dataset to examine 64 publicly reported cases where AI chatbot interactions were linked to severe outcomes, including suicide, psychosis, hospitalization, violence and terrorism. To build the dataset, we systematically gathered cases from news reports and public sources from around the world (US, UK, BE, SE, LB, JP, IN, FI, AR, AU), carefully recording each person's background, chatbot usage, AI model, outcome, and subsequent company response. In our data, 37 cases involve people with no prior psychiatric history, 51 involve ChatGPT, and 29 drew no official comment from the company. We translated this qualitative research into a structured two-dimensional grid pairing psychiatric categories with distinct types of chatbot use. Following a unit chart approach rather than aggregating people into bars or counts, we drew every single case as an individual radial glyph labelled with a name and age group, aiming to highlight their dignity as individuals and to prevent reducing them to a mere statistic. Each glyph's central shape shows the outcome and direction of harm, while three surrounding spokes highlight the AI model, specific condition, and company response. This layered visual layout lets readers read five dimensions at once and seamlessly connect individual human stories to wider systemic patterns. Because our dataset relies entirely on public reporting, it is not a representative statistical sample and shows reported associations rather than definitive proof of causation, serving instead as an urgent call for greater transparency, public accountability, and safer conversational AI design, aswell as increasing awareness in the population on the dangers that this technology might represent.
